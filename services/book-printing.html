Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is PUR Perfect Binding?

PUR uses polyurethane reactive adhesive. It offers twice the pull-strength of traditional hot-melt adhesives and handles thick stocks without pages falling out.

Q: How should I submit files?

Please upload your cover layout as a single spread (with a calculated spine width) and inside pages as sequential single pages.
