We Turn Pixels Into
Real-World Objects.

PRINTXTORE was founded in 2018 with a simple goal: bridge the gap between digital vector precision and beautiful tactile realities. We mix standard paper machinery with highly custom offset finishing, 3D printing, and laser cutting.

Our Mission

To empower designers, businesses, and creators with easy-to-configure, high-end, tactile printing techniques. We believe physical products are an essential communication channel.

Our Vision

To build a globally recognized ecosystem for premium, eco-responsible printing, matching traditional craft with digital-first automated setups.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers about artwork setup, print times, delivery, and refunds.

We strongly recommend vector-based PDF or SVG files with converted curves (outlines) to prevent missing font errors. We also accept high-resolution JPG and PNG files at a minimum resolution of 300 DPI.
Our standard production time is 3–5 working days. We also offer Express 24-Hour processing for select products like standard business cards and simple promotional flyers.
We support major Credit/Debit cards, UPI payments, net banking, and PayPal. For corporate businesses placing bulk volume operations, contact our accounting desk for purchase invoice processing.
Yes! We offer Standard shipping, Express priority courier, Local same-day delivery, and Free studio pickup. Track your delivery status instantly on the customer panel.
If colors differ significantly from your supplied Pantone codes, or if packaging items contain structural errors, we will reprint the batch at no charge or refund your order. Submissions with resolution issues on the customer side are exempt.
