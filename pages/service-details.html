Premium Service Details

Luxury Business Cards

A premium business card does not just convey your contact details—it commands presence and establishes credibility. Our custom-configured business cards are manufactured on high-tonnage Heidelberg offset presses, allowing for beautiful tactile indentations, spot-gloss UV foils, and dense weights.

Service FAQ

What is the standard bleed margin?

We require a 3mm (0.125") bleed margin on all outer edges of your files to ensure layout safety during trimming.

Can I choose custom shapes?

Yes! We provide custom die-cut configurations. Submit details via our custom order panel on the dashboard.
