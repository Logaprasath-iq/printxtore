PRE-PRESS GUIDE

Pre-Press Setup Guide

Configure your document borders, color modes, and typography outlines using these standard rules to prevent errors.

Margins & Dimensions

Trimming blades shift slightly when slicing large printing sheets. By setting up three layered margins, your files remain completely layout-safe.

  • Bleed Line (Red, +3.0mm)

    Extend your background color entirely to this line to prevent white paper borders if cuts shift.

  • Trim Line (White, 0.0mm)

    This is the actual boundary of your finalized product (e.g. 3.5" x 2.0" card size).

  • Safe Boundary (Green, -4.0mm)

    Keep logos, text details, and key vectors inside this line to ensure they never get sliced.

CMYK Color Gamut

Convert files to CMYK (FOGRA39) profiles. RGB profiles (screen displays) contain wide neon gamuts that offset press inks cannot reproduce, leading to flat printed output.

Outline Typography

Select all texts in Adobe Illustrator or InDesign and convert them to Curves/Outlines (Ctrl+Shift+O). This locks fonts into custom vector paths to prevent missing font errors on our presses.

300 DPI Resolution

Export raster graphics and photos at 300 DPI (Dots Per Inch). Screens output at 72 DPI, which looks sharp on screens but renders pixelated or blurry on card stocks.
